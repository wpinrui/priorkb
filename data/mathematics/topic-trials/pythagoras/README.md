# Pythagoras prerequisite trial

Target: use of Pythagoras’ theorem, Secondary 2, G1/G2/G3.

Five distinct questions. Independent candidate discovery, followed by independent assessment of the combined list. Four or five matching votes accept a class; other splits need a user decision.

This is a single-topic trial, not a change to the live prerequisite graph. Inferred subskills are explicitly labelled and have no claimed syllabus introduction year.

## Questions

- **concepts**: Which candidate understandings must already exist for a learner to make sense of the meanings and conditions in a²+b²=c², as distinct from algebraic execution, later applications, or optional proof routes?
- **teaching**: Which diagnostic failure would make a student's explanation of Pythagoras' theorem incoherent, which would make an optional teaching route harder, and which would not obstruct understanding of the new theorem?
- **misconceptions**: For each candidate, what wrong explanation or interpretation does lacking it cause, and is that failure fundamental, route-specific, or unrelated? Judge the exact scope from prerequisite to the Pythagoras target. Consider wrong side, doubling instead of squaring, lengths versus areas, equals-as-command, reversing the theorem, and confusing numerical examples with justification. A prerequisite may be taught or recapped in the same lesson. Classify conceptual essentiality, not mere procedural convenience.
- **routes**: Across valid area/rearrangement explanations, numerical exploration paired with a conceptual account, and algebraic or other reasonable explanations of Pythagoras' theorem, does each candidate provide an essential cross-route prerequisite, a helpful bridge for a chosen route, or no defensible earlier explanatory bridge? Examples alone are not proof, optional proof techniques are not universal requirements, and formula execution is not conceptual understanding.
- **challenge**: For each packet candidate, can a credible learner who lacks that full competence still understand and explain Pythagoras' theorem through another route, or does the candidate supply an unavoidable conceptual foundation? Check for missing core foundations and for converse or coordinate applications that depend on the target rather than precede it.

## Unresolved interpretation issues

- The combined squares-and-roots candidate received 4 essential votes, while conceptual recovery of a side from its square received 2 essential and 3 helpful. The concepts and misconceptions assessors treated side recovery differently between these overlapping candidates. The scope distinction between understanding the identity and using it to find a side remains unresolved. Original votes are preserved.
- The 10 essential classifications are overlapping candidate competencies, not 10 distinct lessons. The teacher-facing summary groups related ideas; it does not combine or manufacture their votes.

Exact scopes, source appearances and all individual rationales are in [assessment.json](assessment.json). Original proposals are preserved in the discovery files. Similar-sounding but differently scoped candidates were not silently merged.

The native agent task identifiers in the manifest are actual task identities. They are not claimed to be Codex CLI session UUIDs. All five assessors used GPT-5.6 Luna. Distinct questions do not guarantee statistical independence or correctness.
